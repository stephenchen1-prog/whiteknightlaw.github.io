Crime Victim Advocacy

You Don't Have to Face This Alone

If you have been affected by a crime, you deserve support, information, and someone to help you navigate the legal and recovery process. Our Crime Victim Advocacy services are here to ensure your rights are protected and that you have access to the resources you need.

About

What Is a Crime Victim Advocate?

A Crime Victim Advocate is a trained professional who provides emotional support, guidance, and practical assistance to individuals who have experienced crime.

Advocates can help victims understand their rights, connect with services, communicate with law enforcement, and navigate the criminal justice system.

Your Rights

Victims Often Have Rights That May Include

  • Being treated with dignity and respect
  • Receiving information about the case
  • Protection from intimidation
  • Participation in court proceedings where permitted
  • Access to victim services
  • Restitution where applicable

Rights vary by state or country. An advocate can help clarify what applies to your situation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service free?

Many of our advocacy services are available at no cost. Contact us to learn about available programs and any applicable fees.

Do I have to report the crime first?

No. You do not need to have filed a police report to receive advocacy support. We assist individuals regardless of whether a report has been made.

Can someone come with me to court?

Yes. Advocates can often accompany victims during court proceedings to provide support and help explain what is happening.

Will you speak to the police for me?

Advocates can help facilitate communication with law enforcement while always respecting your choices and boundaries throughout the process.

Can family members receive support?

In many cases, yes. Family members and caregivers affected by a crime may also be eligible for support services. Ask your advocate for details.
