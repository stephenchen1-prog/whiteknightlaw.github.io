Programs

What White Knight Law does

White Knight Law Inc is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We build free tools and plain-language resources for two groups: the organizations that support crime victims, and the people who have been hurt by fraud, scams and identity theft.

For victim advocacy organizations

Advocates spend hours rebuilding what happened to a client from scattered texts, bank statements and emails. Our tools help them do that work faster and keep it organized.

  • Intake: a consistent way to collect a person's story and documents the first time they reach out.
  • Timelines: events put in order with the records that back them up, ready to share with police, a bank or a lawyer.
  • Records: files kept complete and easy to find, so nothing goes missing when a case moves.
  • Resource finder: public programs and services the people they serve may qualify for.

For people affected by fraud and crime

Fraud leaves people with a pile of paperwork and no clear next step. We help them get organized and find the agencies and organizations that can actually help.

  • Step-by-step guidance: what to do first after fraud or identity theft, and where to report it. See our fraud help page.
  • Getting organized: help putting dates, amounts and messages into one clear record.
  • Connections: pointers to victim advocates, legal aid and public victim services.

How we work

Free, private and plain spoken

Our resources are free for individuals and for nonprofit advocacy organizations. We ask only for the information we need, and we never sell it.

White Knight Law grew out of our founder's own experience with fraud: months spent piecing together what happened before anyone could act on it. Good records change how seriously a case is taken. That is the problem we work on.

We are not a law firm and we do not give legal advice. When someone needs a lawyer, we point them to legal aid and other qualified help.
