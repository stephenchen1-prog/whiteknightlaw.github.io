About White Knight Law Inc

White Knight Law Inc is a nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding access to legal services, community advocacy, education, and technology-driven public benefit initiatives.

White Knight Law Inc owns and operates whiteknightlaw.org as its official website and communication platform.

All programs, services, communications, and online resources provided through whiteknightlaw.org are operated by White Knight Law Inc.

Leadership & Governance

Leadership

Director of Social Impact

Stephen Chen

Stephen Chen is committed to advancing community impact through education, civic engagement, public service, and initiatives that expand opportunity and access to resources for underserved communities.

His work and philanthropic activities have been featured by The Wall Street Journal, The New York Times, The Washington Post, Fox News, ABC, and NBC. He has spoken at Yale University, Brown University, New York University, and Columbia University on leadership, innovation, entrepreneurship, and the future of business.

He is a member of the U.S. Chamber of Commerce in New York City, the Urban Land Institute, and the New York Investment Network. His community efforts have been recognized through the Orphans International Global Citizenship Award, the Dragon 100 Fellowship, and the C.V. Starr Fellowship. Stephen attended Brown University and New York University.

As Director of Social Impact, Stephen supports initiatives focused on access to justice, legal education, community advocacy, civic engagement, and technology-enabled public benefit programs.

Advisory Board

Senior Advisor

Dr. Larry A. Jones

Dr. Larry A. Jones is a physician, author, educator, nonprofit leader, and recipient of CHADD's Lifetime Achievement Award. Advisor

Adam Ocner

Adam Ocner is an investigator, legal professional, and advisor with experience in criminal investigations, financial investigations, surveillance operations, and evidence collection. He holds a Juris Doctor degree and completed federal law enforcement training through FLETC.

His professional experience includes investigative work involving complex criminal and financial matters, organized crime investigations, RICO-related cases, witness location, fraud investigations, and litigation support. He previously served as an investigator in matters involving the United States Department of Justice and has worked extensively with law firms, insurance companies, and law enforcement agencies.

As an advisor to White Knight Law Inc, Adam provides guidance on investigations, organizational development, risk assessment, stakeholder engagement, and strategic initiatives that support the organization's public-interest mission.

Governance and Transparency

White Knight Law Inc is a nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding access to legal education, community advocacy, conflict resolution resources, and technology-driven public benefit initiatives.

The organization is supported by leaders, board members, advisors, volunteers, and community partners who share a commitment to expanding access to justice, education, civic engagement, and public-interest resources.

White Knight Law Inc develops educational programs, community initiatives, legal information resources, technology platforms, and strategic partnerships that help individuals and communities access information, services, and opportunities.

White Knight Law Inc owns and operates whiteknightlaw.org as its official website and communication platform.

White Knight Law Inc is recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a tax-exempt nonprofit organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. EIN: 42-1827954.
